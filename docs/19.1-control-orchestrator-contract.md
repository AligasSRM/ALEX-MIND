# Section 19.1 — ALEX Control Orchestrator Contract

Status: ACTIVE

## Purpose
Define ALEX as the central control and orchestration layer over ALEX MIND.

ALEX coordinates authorized operations across sources, vaults, storage, projects, workspaces, ingestion, knowledge, synchronization, and monitoring.

## Responsibilities
ALEX may coordinate:
- source connections and connector operations;
- data intake and routing;
- object storage and indexing;
- project/workspace operations;
- knowledge processing and retrieval;
- synchronization;
- health and failure handling;
- audit and operational state.

ALEX is the control plane, not the storage location for all data.

## Authority boundary
ALEX may initiate or coordinate an operation only when the required authorization and policy conditions are satisfied.

ALEX must not bypass source permissions, project isolation, vault policy, security policy, or connector restrictions.

## Secret boundary
ALEX must never expose, persist, print, or intentionally retrieve:
- passwords;
- API secrets;
- private keys;
- wallet seeds;
- authentication tokens;
- other secret credential values.

Connectors may perform authenticated operations through their secure credential mechanisms while exposing only safe status and operation results to the control layer.

## Source independence
Each source remains independently identifiable. ALEX may coordinate multiple sources without merging their identities or credentials.

## Operation model
Every controlled operation should have:
- operation identity;
- initiating source or project;
- target resource;
- requested action;
- authorization context;
- policy decision;
- lifecycle state;
- result or failure state;
- timestamps;
- audit reference.

## Fail-closed behavior
If authorization, scope, required policy, connector state, or resource identity cannot be established, ALEX must not guess or continue with an unsafe operation.

## Implementation boundary
This contract defines the control-plane boundary only. It does not itself create a credential store or grant access to any external service.

## Acceptance criteria
- ALEX is defined as the central orchestrator.
- Responsibilities cover the complete ALEX MIND operating surface.
- Secrets remain outside readable control data.
- Authorization and project/vault boundaries are mandatory.
- Operations are traceable.
- Unsafe uncertainty fails closed.
