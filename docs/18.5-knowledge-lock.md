# Section 18.5 — Knowledge Lock

Status: GREEN / CLOSED / LOCKED

## Scope
This lock closes Section 18 Knowledge after completion of:
- 18.1 Knowledge Contract;
- 18.2 Knowledge Lifecycle;
- 18.3 Knowledge Classification & Confidence;
- 18.4 Knowledge Relationships & Retrieval.

## Locked guarantees
Section 18 establishes that:
- raw intake is distinct from knowledge;
- knowledge has stable identity and explicit scope;
- source provenance remains traceable;
- lifecycle states and transitions are explicit;
- verified, derived, disputed, unverified, and obsolete states are distinguishable;
- confidence is evidence-based and never invented;
- source authority is distinct from confidence;
- temporal validity is represented;
- relationships preserve provenance;
- retrieval respects security, authorization, project scope, lifecycle, and temporal constraints before relevance;
- conflicts remain visible;
- project-specific knowledge remains isolated;
- historical knowledge remains traceable;
- secrets are excluded from knowledge and retrieval;
- missing required provenance or authorization fails safely.

## Section boundary
Section 18 is an architecture/documentation lock.

No runtime knowledge engine, automatic classification, vector database, search service, or new database schema is implied by this lock.

## Regression boundary
Sections 1–17 remain closed.

This lock does not authorize reopening, rewriting, or refactoring previously locked sections.

## Reopen rule
Section 18 may be reopened only when a concrete technical defect, security issue, architectural contradiction, or verified requirement change is identified.

A preference change or speculative improvement alone does not reopen the lock.

## Final status
GREEN — completed
CLOSED — implementation boundary fixed
LOCKED — no further changes without a justified reopen condition
