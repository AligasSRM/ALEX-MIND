# Section 19.4 — Control Audit, Health & Recovery

Status: ACTIVE

## Purpose
Define the operational visibility ALEX needs to supervise the whole system while keeping secrets outside ordinary telemetry.

## Audit
Every significant controlled operation should produce an audit record containing safe metadata such as:
- operation ID;
- source/project/vault scope;
- action type;
- target identity;
- policy decision;
- start/end time;
- outcome;
- failure category;
- retry/recovery state.

Audit records must not contain passwords, API secrets, private keys, wallet seeds, or raw authentication tokens.

## Health
ALEX should monitor:
- connector availability;
- authorization state;
- storage backend availability;
- D1/KV availability;
- ingestion state;
- workspace state;
- knowledge processing state;
- synchronization state;
- recent failures;
- pending operations.

Health describes system state; it does not grant authorization.

## Recovery
Recovery should distinguish:
- retryable failures;
- non-retryable failures;
- authorization blocks;
- policy blocks;
- provider outages;
- data integrity failures;
- reconciliation-required states.

Recovery must preserve idempotency and audit history.

## Alerts
Operational alerts should identify what failed and where, without exposing the secret that caused or authenticated the operation.

## Reconciliation
When a remote operation may have succeeded but local confirmation is missing, ALEX must enter a reconciliation state rather than blindly repeating the operation.

## Lock safety
Locked or archived projects/workspaces must not receive normal active writes through recovery automation.

## Implementation boundary
This document defines audit, health, and recovery semantics only. It does not create monitoring infrastructure or automatic recovery jobs.

## Acceptance criteria
- Significant operations are auditable with safe metadata.
- Health covers the complete ALEX MIND operating surface.
- Recovery distinguishes failure classes.
- Uncertain remote outcomes enter reconciliation.
- Secrets remain excluded from telemetry.
- Recovery respects project/workspace locks.
