# 7.4 SYNC — Failure Handling

## Rules
1. Detect and record failure.
2. Preserve already-synchronized data.
3. Do not silently discard new data.
4. Isolate the affected source or backend.
5. Retry only through controlled policy.
6. Keep failure state observable.

## Principle
A failure in one source or provider must not corrupt unrelated vaults or projects.
