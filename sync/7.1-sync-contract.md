# 7.1 SYNC — Sync Contract

## Purpose
Defines controlled movement of data between sources, the central vault, storage backends, and optional phone surfaces.

## Rules
- Sync is policy-driven.
- Vault Sync is the primary synchronization path.
- Phone Sync is optional.
- Every sync operation preserves source identity.
- Secrets are never synchronized into repository files.
